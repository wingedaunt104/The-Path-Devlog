# The Path - Changelog

This file tracks major development milestones, gameplay systems, improvements, and fixes made during the development of The Path.

## September 2026

- Developed **Storming Peaks** from its initial environment prototype into a fully storm-driven parkour level: created volumetric storm clouds, atmospheric lighting, lower cloud layers, dynamic lightning flashes, Niagara lightning bolts, randomized background and parkour strike locations, blue environmental flames, floating rock formations, ruins, and custom modular wall-running surfaces; expanded and refined the main traversal route while improving level readability, fog, lighting, cloud density, environmental composition, and the Blender-to-Unreal static-mesh workflow; built a complete lightning hazard system with localized 3D warning audio, player-to-strike distance detection, lethal blast zones, directional knockback, ragdoll integration, physics-based lightning death impulses, and fallback projectile death behavior; created randomized close and distant thunder sound pools with distance-based selection and realistic thunder delay; added GPU-simulated player-following rain with looping ambience and automatic reattachment after respawn; created Niagara lightning impact explosions, electric flashes, and outward energy streak effects; improved storm pacing, thunder variety, spatial feedback, hazard readability, environmental audio, Niagara performance, ragdoll physics, and per-hazard death impulses; fixed rain reattachment after respawn, projectile respawn flow after custom death impulses, incorrect world-direction ragdoll launches, and repeating Niagara impact effects; and added moving-platform reset/restart logic so parkour platforms can return to their starting state and become usable again after player death and respawn.
